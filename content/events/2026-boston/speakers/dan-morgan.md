Dan is a native New Englander, real salt of the earth kinda guy. He grew up in New Hampshire where the Saturday Night Moose Fights thrive in cold winter months. As the former captain of the New Hampshire Moose Fighting team the Mountain Men; he was widely known for his strength, prowess and skill in fighting the deadliest of New England moose. Simply speaking his name aloud often struck fear and bouts of inconsolable crying in his opponents.

Dan has more than 25 years in the industry, much of which was spent in and around the Boston area as a contractor. Known as a complex problem solver, he has worked with brands both big and small such as Morningstar, Virgin Mobile USA, Mini USA, PUMA, FedEX and Greater Boston Food Bank. Currently working as Senior Architect in the exciting world of taxes.. He is actively engages in AI initiatives leading towards AI SDLC, security and mentoring the yutes.
