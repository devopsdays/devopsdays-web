Niharika P. Pujari is a Lead Software Engineer with over nine years of experience building and scaling production software systems. Her work focuses on frontend architecture, cloud-native applications, CI/CD automation, software reliability, accessibility engineering, and responsible use of AI-assisted development tools. She has published and presented work on production engineering, accessibility, and AI-assisted software development at IEEE, ACM, and industry venues. She is also an IEEE Senior Member and ACM Senior Member.
