Pravalik Medi manages enterprise database operations for a national restaurant company, running production estates across Amazon RDS, EC2, on-premise SQL Server, MySQL, and Oracle. His work focuses on operational reliability at scale, cost reduction in cloud database infrastructure, and getting teams out of reactive incident response. He writes about database operations practice and lives in San Diego.
