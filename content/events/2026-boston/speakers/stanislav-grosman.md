Stanislav Grosman is a DevOps and Platform Engineering leader with 6+ years of experience building and operating production infrastructure. His work spans cloud platforms, Kubernetes, CI/CD, infrastructure automation, observability, and developer platforms.

He enjoys turning complex, distributed infrastructure into systems that are easier to understand, operate, and scale - and is particularly interested in the intersection of engineering, reliability, and organizational complexity.
