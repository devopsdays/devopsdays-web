Aaron Krauss is a Technical Lead at Clevyr based in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, with more than 15 years of experience in software development. He specializes in building workflow-intensive applications using technologies such as Laravel, Vue, Tailwind CSS, Flutter, Ruby, Go, Python, SQL, Swift, Docker, CI/CD, and Kubernetes. Aaron is a strong advocate for Spec-Driven Development and enjoys leveraging AI-assisted development tools such as Claude Code and Cursor to improve engineering productivity and software quality.

Beyond his professional work, Aaron is a dedicated leader in Oklahoma’s technology community. He organizes OKC WebDevs, one of Oklahoma City’s largest developer meetup groups, and has been an active contributor to Techlahoma, a nonprofit technology organization with more than 8,000 members, for over a decade. Throughout that time, he has served as a speaker, organizer, and board member, helping foster collaboration and growth within the regional developer ecosystem.

Aaron is also the creator of personal projects including Simple Slides and The Krauss Haus Bar Menu. Outside of technology, he enjoys brewing tea, crafting cocktails, longboarding, indulging in Nutella, and spending time with his wife and two sons.
