Chaitanya Anne is a Tech Lead Cloud DevOps Engineer at Southwest Airlines, specializing in cybersecurity, cloud platform engineering, and observability. His expertise lies in designing and implementing scalable, cloud-native observability platforms using OpenTelemetry, Terraform, and AWS to improve reliability, strengthen security, and enhance operational efficiency across large-scale distributed systems.

Throughout his career, Chaitanya has led initiatives spanning enterprise observability, AI-driven automation for security operations, identity and access management (IAM) modernization, and Zero Trust architecture. He is particularly passionate about the responsible adoption of Generative AI in production environments, exploring how intelligent automation can improve incident response, accelerate root cause analysis, and reduce mean time to resolution.

Chaitanya is focused on bridging the gap between infrastructure telemetry and intelligent automation, enabling organizations to transition from reactive incident response to proactive, AI-assisted operations. His work centers on building resilient, secure, and observable cloud platforms that empower engineering teams to detect, diagnose, and respond to operational challenges with greater speed, accuracy, and confidence.
