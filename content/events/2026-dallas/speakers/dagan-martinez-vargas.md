Dagan Martinez-Vargas is a keynote speaker, founder, and author of Play the Room, a practical guide to turning everyday conversations into real business opportunities. He helps professionals become more memorable, more confident, and more strategic in how they build relationships, communicate their value, and create opportunity through intentional connection. Drawing from his background in theater, advanced leadership training through Toastmasters International, and years of experience in sales, brand representation, and business development, Dagan delivers engaging, practical presentations audiences can apply immediately. He has spoken at chamber events, networking events, sales environments, and business conferences, equipping professionals with tools to strengthen communication, build authentic connections, and create measurable results.
