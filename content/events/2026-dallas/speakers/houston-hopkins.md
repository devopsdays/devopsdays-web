Houston Hopkins is the CISO at Abnormal AI, an AI-native behavioral security company. He leads the company's security strategy, with a focus on infrastructure safety, identity-centric architecture, and federal compliance. He is also an organizer and board member for fwd:cloudsec, a community focused on cloud security research and knowledge sharing.
