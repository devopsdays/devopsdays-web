Lorenzo Samaniego is a Cloud Services Manager at GM Financial and a senior IT professional specializing in cloud-native technologies and DevOps principles and practices. He is passionate about driving continuous improvement through collaboration, team empowerment, and the pursuit of knowledge.

Throughout his career, Lorenzo has focused on helping teams adopt modern cloud and DevOps approaches that improve delivery, reliability, and operational effectiveness. He champions environments where people are encouraged to share ideas, strengthen their skills, and work together to create sustainable technology outcomes.
