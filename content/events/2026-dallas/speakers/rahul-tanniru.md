Rahul Tanniru is a Senior Vice President of Software Engineering at JPMorgan Chase, where he specializes in enterprise-scale cloud platforms, distributed systems, and AI-enabled infrastructure. He leads the design and delivery of secure, scalable, and highly resilient cloud-native architectures that support mission-critical financial systems.

His expertise spans AWS, Kubernetes, platform engineering, DevOps, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), with a strong focus on building self-service infrastructure platforms that enable engineering organizations to operate efficiently at scale. Rahul has extensive experience designing multi-account cloud architectures, automating infrastructure, improving platform reliability, and integrating AI and machine learning workloads into modern cloud environments.

With more than a decade of experience in enterprise technology, Rahul has architected high-performance systems that enhance scalability, operational efficiency, and resilience across complex cloud environments. He is passionate about advancing cloud architecture standards, mentoring engineering teams, evaluating emerging technologies, and contributing to the broader technology community through conference speaking, technical publications, and industry judging initiatives.
