Shiva Krishna Kodithyala is a Senior Engineering Manager with over 20 years of experience in platform engineering, Quality Engineering (QE) and DevOps transformation. He has led large-scale initiatives to build scalable CI/CD platforms, enhance test reliability, and drive engineering productivity in the fintech industry. Shiva is a published author focused on the intersection of AI and quality engineering, with a strong interest in applying AI to modern software delivery systems. A frequent speaker, mentor, and judge, he is passionate about helping organizations evolve toward intelligent, resilient, and high-performing engineering practices.
