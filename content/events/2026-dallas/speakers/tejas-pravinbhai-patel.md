With a career spanning cloud-native platforms, backend infrastructure, and AI-driven systems, Tejas is recognized for his ability to bridge the gap between cutting-edge research and real-world engineering at scale.

His award-winning research has been presented at leading IEEE conferences and journals, advancing the state of the art in AI reliability, scalability, and operational intelligence.

A sought-after keynote speaker at top-tier technology conferences, Tejas delivers compelling, insight-rich presentations that challenge conventional thinking and inspire engineers to build better, more trustworthy AI systems. His keynotes are known for combining deep technical depth with practical, actionable takeaways drawn from production experience at Amazon scale.

Tejas specializes in AI agents, multi-agent orchestration, LLM-powered applications, distributed systems reliability, and cloud-native AI deployment. He is passionate about making AI systems production-ready — moving beyond benchmark performance to operational excellence in real-world environments.

Whether speaking on stage or publishing groundbreaking research, Tejas brings a rare combination of industry expertise, academic rigor, and communication excellence that makes him one of the most impactful voices in AI and Cloud engineering today.
