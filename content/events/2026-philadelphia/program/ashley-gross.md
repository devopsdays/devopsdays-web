The interesting question about an agent in production is not what it can do. It is what it is allowed to do at 2am on a Saturday, and who answers for it on Monday.

I have deployed production AI agents at Microsoft, Abbott, Visa and Unilever, generating more than $3 million in annualized savings across 32-plus integrated systems. The deployments that scaled were not the ones with the best models. They were the ones where somebody decided, in advance and in writing, which actions required a human signature and which did not.

This session is the operational version of that. How to segment a real workflow into agent-suitable and human-only steps. Where to place approval gates so they catch failures without strangling throughput. What to instrument so you can actually tell the thing is working. And the over-automation mistakes that force a rollback six months in, which are almost always scoped in on day one.

Aimed at people who already have agents running or are about to. You will leave with a way to define agent boundaries and a clear read on which of your processes are genuinely ready.
