The deployment of agentic systems, and their use in development, has brought a radical shift to our industry. These systems are not simply new applications to deploy into existing fleets, but bring fresh operational challenges. We will explore what these systems are, how agentic systems work, how they are changing DevOps, and the new security challenges posed by these systems.
