Ajay is the author of 3 highly popular Platform Engineering books: The Platform Engineer's Handbook, co-author of Effective Platform Engineering, and Domain-Driven Platform Engineering. His current work focuses on the intersection of platform engineering and agentic AI: how Internal Developer Platforms evolve into Agentic Platforms, and what that means for golden paths, governance, and the teams that build them.
