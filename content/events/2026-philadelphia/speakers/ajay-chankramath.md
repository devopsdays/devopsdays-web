Ajay Chankramath is the Founder and CEO of Platformetrics, a platform engineering advisory firm based in Colorado. He leads advisory, training, and certification programs across communities such as PlatformEngineering.org that help enterprise teams design, build, and scale developer platforms that deliver measurable business outcomes, bringing together his nearly 4 decades of experience in software architecture and development.

Ajay is the author of 3 highly popular Platform Engineering books: The Platform Engineer's Handbook, co-author of Effective Platform Engineering, and Domain-Driven Platform Engineering. His current work focuses on the intersection of platform engineering and agentic AI: how Internal Developer Platforms evolve into Agentic Platforms, and what that means for golden paths, governance, and the teams that build them.
