Anish Puthuraya leads data engineering at Pyramid Global Hospitality - a major US hospitality management company, where he owns enterprise data architecture across a 150+ property portfolio.

His work covers production ML forecasting on Microsoft Fabric - booking-curve demand models, blended statistical and ML forecasts, and full nightly P&L assembly, along with governed lakehouse platforms and human-in-the-loop LLM systems for finance. He has led cloud analytics work at PwC and enterprise Power Platform delivery at Wipro.

He holds an M.S. in Business Analytics from Boston University Questrom and a B.Tech. from IIT Bombay.
