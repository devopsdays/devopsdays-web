Hi, I'm Chris, and I'm currently working as an SRE and an open-source and cloud-native advocate in the defense industry.

I am a CNCF Ambassador and contribute to its community and chapters in several ways. I am involved with the Kubernetes project, specifically in its contributor communications sub-project, and I represent the Cloud Native NYC and Santo Domingo chapters of the CNCF. I am also an organizer for the Kubernetes Community Days in NYC.

I enjoy helping others advance their careers in the tech industry. I strongly believe that a few words of encouragement can significantly impact someone's life, just as they did for me when I began my career.
