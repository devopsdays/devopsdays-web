With everything changing in tech at a frenetic pace, the emphasis on developer productivity has overshadowed the true essence of developer experience (DevEx). Frameworks like SPACE, getDX, and DORA metrics provide valuable insights, but agentic coding tools have broken their core assumption: that a human is the one typing. When agents can generate a hundred story points in an hour, "velocity" stops meaning anything. This talk explores how the fundamentals of DevEx hold up in an agentic world, why the friction hasn't disappeared but *moved*, from writing code to reviewing, trusting, and governing it, and what a practitioner-centric measurement approach looks like when your teammate might be an agent.
