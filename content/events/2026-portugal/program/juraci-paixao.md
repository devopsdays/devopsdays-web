For fifteen years, we designed telemetry for humans reading dashboards. Today, coding agents and incident response agents read it first, and they take every log line at face value. This keynote draws on a year of conversations with platform teams to show what breaks when agents consume bad telemetry, what machine-readable telemetry looks like, and why trusting the agent starts with trusting the data.
