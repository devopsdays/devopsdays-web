Five years ago, we joined a journey to create an Internal Developer Platform with an ambitious goal: accelerate delivery, reduce developer cognitive load, and enable teams through self-service.

What began as a collection of infrastructure services evolved into a product mindset focused on users, adoption, experience, and measurable outcomes.
In this talk, I'll share the real lessons learned from transforming platform engineering into product engineering:

- Why technology was never the hardest problem
- How we shifted from infrastructure delivery to customer-centric platform development
- Mistakes we made while building self-service capabilities
- Measuring platform success through adoption instead of feature count
- Reducing cognitive load and improving developer experience at scale
- Balancing standardization with developer autonomy
