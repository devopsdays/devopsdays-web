Colin Lacy is a Lead Software Engineer in Cisco Meraki's Hardware team, and a life-long believer in leveraging automation to standardize and simplify everyday tasks. He is an active mentor to junior developers, and has taught multiple software bootcamps, ranging from object-oriented programming to Kubernetes – how to use it, and how it works under the hood.
