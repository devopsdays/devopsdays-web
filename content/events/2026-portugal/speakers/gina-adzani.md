Gina is a developer and maintainer of Traefik. After studying civil engineering and economics, she pivoted into tech as a frontend developer, and then made the leap into Traefik Proxy's core, driven by curiosity and a love for exploring new corners of the stack. She's passionate about open source and the fast-moving cloud native landscape, always up for the next "wait, how does this work" moment. Away from the keyboard, you'll find her exploring the outdoors or experimenting in the kitchen.
