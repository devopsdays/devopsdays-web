Ilia Dubovskii is the founder of Beamreach, where he builds autonomous systems for DevOps, cloud security, and enterprise automation. With over a decade of experience in cloud infrastructure and DevSecOps, he has designed and operated large-scale AWS and Azure environments across startups and global enterprises. His current work focuses on autonomous security, AI agents, and practical automation that helps engineering teams reduce operational overhead while improving security. He is the creator of Beamreach Compass, an autonomous DevOps platform featuring capabilities such as Canary Honeypots, automated security analysis, and cloud optimisation.
