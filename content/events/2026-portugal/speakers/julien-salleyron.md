Julien Salleyron is a Principal Engineer at Traefik Labs and a core maintainer of Traefik—the popular open-source cloud-native application proxy. For the past 10+ years, he’s been immersed in the world of microservices, containers, and distributed systems—speaking Go fluently and Kubernetes natively. Most recently, Julien has been leading efforts at Traefik Labs to design and build a new AI Gateway—bringing the principles of cloud-native networking to the fast-evolving landscape of LLMs.
