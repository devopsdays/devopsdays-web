Mario Fahlandt is a CNCF TOC Member, Kubernetes SIG ContribEx Co-Chair, and Lead of the Kubernetes AI Conformance subproject. He is a primary maintainer of cncf/sbom, generating SBOMs for all CNCF projects, and created BOMHort, an open source SBOM governance platform. Mario serves on the Google Developer Advisory Board and is a CNCF and Arm Ambassador. He writes Last Week in Cloud Native (lwcn.dev), a weekly newsletter tracking the cloud native ecosystem. By day, he works as Customer Delivery Architect at Kubermatic.
