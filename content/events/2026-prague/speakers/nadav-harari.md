Nadav Harary is a senior SEO specialist with more than 12 years of experience across technical SEO, content strategy, automation, and international search. He has worked with brands including Trezor and Lingoda, Norwegian Cruise Line, helping teams improve organic visibility through scalable systems, data analysis, and close collaboration with developers. His work often sits at the intersection of SEO and engineering, from automated monitoring and crawling to release validation and indexability checks. He is also building LazySEM, a platform focused on practical automation tools for SEO professionals.
