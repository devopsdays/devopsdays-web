Szymon Izydorek is a DevOps Engineer at Thales Group, where he focuses on configuration management, infrastructure automation and optimization. 

He is also the founder of BecomeDevops, a company dedicated to helping engineers enter the IT industry and grow their skills. Through BecomeDevops, he teaches Ansible automation, Docker Security, and Kubernetes

. Szymon regularly shares his expertise through free-to-watch content on online platforms and at conferences such as DevOpsDays, covering topics including Ansible, Linux, certifications, and DevOps.
