DevOpsDays Baltimore 2027 will be held at **The Garage at R. House**, an event space adjacent to the [R. House food hall](https://r.housebaltimore.com/) in Baltimore's Remington neighborhood.

**The Garage at R. House**
301 W 29th St
Baltimore, MD 21211

[Get directions to R. House](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=R.+House%2C+301+W+29th+St%2C+Baltimore%2C+MD+21211).

### Parking

R. House has a number of parking options, broadly available street parking, and provides validation for two Remington garages.  More details will be added closer to the event.
