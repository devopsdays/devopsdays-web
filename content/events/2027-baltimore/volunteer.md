<h2>Become a Volunteer</h2>
<p>Help bring DevOpsDays Baltimore 2027 to life at The Garage at R. House! We bring together development, operations, InfoSec, AI, Quality Assurance, IT management, and leadership to share experiences and learn from one another.</p>
<p>We're looking for volunteers to help in the months leading up to the conference and during the event itself. Whether you're a student, an experienced practitioner, or new to the community, we'd love to hear from you.</p>

<div class="row">
  <div class="col-md-6">
    <h3>Why Volunteer?</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Meet the community:</strong> Connect with practitioners, speakers, and organizers from Baltimore and the broader DMV technology community.</li>
      <li><strong>Learn together:</strong> Share experiences and discover how other teams approach DevOps challenges.</li>
      <li><strong>Build experience:</strong> Develop skills in event planning, communication, and collaboration.</li>
      <li><strong>Give back:</strong> Help create a welcoming event where people can connect and exchange ideas.</li>
    </ul>
    <h3>Before the Event</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Marketing and social media:</strong> Help spread the word and share conference updates.</li>
      <li><strong>Sponsor outreach:</strong> Connect with organizations interested in supporting the community.</li>
      <li><strong>Community and student outreach:</strong> Reach local meetups, universities, bootcamps, and technology groups.</li>
      <li><strong>Speaker coordination:</strong> Assist with speaker communications and logistics.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="col-md-6">
    <h3>During the Event</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Registration and check-in:</strong> Welcome attendees, distribute badges, and help people find their way.</li>
      <li><strong>Session support:</strong> Assist speakers and help keep sessions running smoothly.</li>
      <li><strong>Technical support:</strong> Help with audiovisual setup and troubleshooting.</li>
      <li><strong>Open Spaces:</strong> Help attendees propose topics and find discussions.</li>
      <li><strong>Attendee experience:</strong> Answer questions and connect attendees with organizers when they need assistance.</li>
      <li><strong>Event logistics:</strong> Help with setup, cleanup, and tasks throughout the day.</li>
    </ul>
    <h3>Get Involved</h3>
    <div class="alert alert-info">
      <p>Interested in volunteering? Email {{< email_organizers >}} with your interests and availability. We'll work with you to find a way to help.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
