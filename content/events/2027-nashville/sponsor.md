## Why sponsor DevOpsDays Nashville?

DevOpsDays Nashville 2027 brings together the local and regional DevOps community for two days in Murfreesboro, Tennessee. Sponsoring helps keep the event accessible while putting your brand in front of an engaged technical audience.

Sponsors can expect opportunities to:

* Reach DevOps, cloud, platform engineering, SRE, security, software delivery, and technical leadership practitioners.
* Support a community-driven event with an audience that values learning over sales pitches.
* Build awareness through event website placement, onsite visibility, sponsor recognition, and community promotion.
* Meet attendees during breaks, meals, sponsor moments, and open spaces.
* Send technical team members who can have useful conversations with customers, candidates, partners, and peers.

The 2027 Nashville event is planned as a two-day conference with 20+ speakers and 250+ attendees. It is 100% community-run, and sponsor support is what makes that possible.
